2. Record a receiver location

Keep the tag stationary. Move only the receiver. Wait for two complete stable scan windows, then enter RSSI_AVG.


One reading gives a distance. Three or more distinct locations, spread across the room, enable a position fit. Start a new survey whenever the tag moves.

How the prototype works

One ESP advertises BLE packets. The second averages received signal strength over three seconds. A calibrated log-distance model converts RSSI into range. After the receiver visits at least three non-collinear coordinates, nonlinear least squares finds the stationary tag position that best matches those ranges within the room.

Future extension: put a receiver at each location for simultaneous measurements and moving-tag tracking. The existing multi-anchor firmware remains available.
